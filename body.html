ORGAN / STRUCTURE

Heart (Cor)

1

NORMAL FUNCTION

The heart is a four-chambered muscular pump that drives continuous circulation throughout the entire human body. It propels deoxygenated blood to the pulmonary circulation for gas exchange and forcefully ejects oxygenated blood through the aorta into the systemic arterial tree.

2

CARDIOVASCULAR CONNECTION

As the central engine of the vascular system, the heart generates systolic pressure and sustains capillary perfusion pressure across every organ. Chronic elevated afterload (high blood pressure) requires the left ventricle to pump harder, which can lead to ventricular wall remodeling and increased myocardial oxygen demand.

3

RELEVANT PATIENT FACTORS

Evaluated from your submitted profile: Blood Pressure, Cholesterol, Fasting Blood Sugar, BMI, and Lifestyle Habits.

4

WHAT THIS MEANS

Your heart is directly influenced by the systemic resistance of your blood vessels and the oxygen-carrying capacity of your blood. Proactive blood pressure control, physical conditioning, and smoke-free living help minimize cardiac strain and support long-term heart muscle health.
