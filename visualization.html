ANATOMICAL FOCUS

Whole Heart (Cor)

The human heart is a muscular organ with four chambers that pumps blood through the circulatory system. Deoxygenated blood returns from the body into the right atrium, travels to the right ventricle, and is pumped to the lungs. Oxygen-rich blood returns to the left atrium, moves into the left ventricle, and is propelled through the aorta to sustain all body tissues.

2. GENERAL EDUCATIONAL INTERPRETATIONS

Cardiovascular health depends on the balance between cardiac muscle strength, vessel lumen diameter, blood pressure, and metabolic fuel regulation. Maintaining target blood pressure (<120/80 mmHg) and healthy lipid balance protects endothelial lining from cumulative mechanical stress.

3. SCHEMATIC VISUALIZATION EFFECTS

Any glowing color highlights, arterial glow pulses, or pressure indications rendered on the 3D heart are illustrative schematic models designed to assist visual understanding of hemodynamic principles.
