MANDATORY MEDICAL ACCURACY NOTICE

TSVP — The Smart Vascular Predictor is an educational technology demonstration and research prototype developed for educational and hackathon exploration. This system is not a certified medical device and does not provide medical diagnoses, treatment prescriptions, or cardiovascular risk predictions.

  • No Diagnosis: This report cannot diagnose hypertension, coronary artery disease, atherosclerosis, or diabetes mellitus from self-reported survey values.
  • Prescription Integrity: Never alter, reduce, or stop any prescribed medications (including blood pressure, cholesterol, or blood glucose medicines) without direct supervision and authorization from your prescribing physician.
  • Emergency Guidance: If you or anyone around you experiences potential symptoms of acute myocardial infarction, severe angina, sudden dyspnea, or stroke (facial drooping, arm weakness, speech difficulty), call 911 / 999 / 112 immediately.
  • Clinical Interpretation: All physiological measurements must be evaluated within the context of a comprehensive medical examination by a licensed medical practitioner.
